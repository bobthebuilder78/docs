Your account belongs to your organization, not to you individually. Once your organization is approved, every team member you invite works within that same organization, with access scoped to whatever role you assign them.

Your Jaspari Studies account belongs to your organization, not to an individual. Once the organization is approved and the Business Associate Agreement is in place, you can invite colleagues, assign roles, and start designing studies without waiting for a sales or implementation team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Visit [https://studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup) and submit your organization details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await organization approval">
    Jaspari reviews your submission to verify your organization. You will receive notification once approval is granted and login is enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute the BAA">
    Sign the Business Associate Agreement at the organization level. This single agreement covers all studies and users under your organization and satisfies HIPAA requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and invite your team">
    Once approved, log in and begin inviting team members. Assign roles and study-level permissions so each person sees only what they need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite Your Team Getting your account live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup) and complete the organization application with your organization's details and contact information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Jaspari reviews your application before you can log in. You'll be notified once it's approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute your BAA">
    A Business Associate Agreement is executed at the organization level, covering every study you run under it. Your team doesn't need to sign a separate agreement per study.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and invite your team">
    Once approved, log in and start inviting team members. See [Roles & Permissions](/organizations/roles-permissions) for the five roles available and what each one grants.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After setup, invite the people who will work on your studies. Typical roles include:

- **Recruiters**: manage studies, eligibility, and participant outreach.
- **Legal / compliance**: review BAA status, audit logs, and IRB materials.
- **Site coordinators**: manage site-level enrollment and volunteer routing.
- **Sponsor representatives**: read-only access to spend monitoring and study progress.

You can also invite CROs, sponsors, and collaborating sites with scoped permissions per user, role, study, or site.

## Organizations that collaborate

Jaspari Studies supports three organization types. Choose the one that matches your structure:

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Autonomous Clinic" icon="hospital" href="/organizations/roles-permissions">
    A single-site clinic running its own recruitment. Manage users and permissions directly within your organization.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Research Organization" icon="flask" href="/organizations/collaboration">
    A sponsor, CRO, or research group that collaborates with external sites. Invite partners and assign read-only or scoped access.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Site Network" icon="network-wired" href="/organizations/site-networks">
    A multi-site network that needs centralized control with per-site enrollment targets and local site coordinators.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Some organizations run entirely on their own: one organization, one team, its own studies and sites. Others work as part of a larger arrangement, an individual clinic collaborating on studies a sponsor or CRO owns, or a clinic in a network of sites recruiting for a central organization's studies.

There's no separate account type for any of this. Every organization on Jaspari Studies is the same kind of account. What differs is whether it owns its studies outright or collaborates on studies another organization owns, and at what tier. See [Collaboration](/organizations/collaboration) for how that works, and [Site Networks](/organizations/site-networks) for the specific pattern of one organization owning studies that several separately-run clinics collaborate on.
