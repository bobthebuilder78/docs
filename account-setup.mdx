A single organization operates as an administrative unit for studies, sites, and team members. Both independent organizations and collaborators on multi-organization studies maintain the same account structure.

Every organization on the Studies Platform is the same kind of account, whether it's a clinic, a sponsor, a CRO, or a multi-site network.

**Execution:**

1. Navigate to [studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup).
2. Enter your name, email, phone number, and password. Accept the Service Agreement. Select **Continue**.
3. Enter the verification code sent to your email.
4. Enter your organization name, select an organization type (individual clinic, clinic network, sponsor, CRO, academic institution, government agency, or other), and enter your organization website. Select **Submit**.
5. The platform transitions organization status to Pending Review.

**Verification:** Receive an approval email at the address you registered with. Log in to the platform. Organization status displays as Active on the Dashboard. You can now create studies and invite team members.

## Team authorization

**Objective:** Configure role-based access for team members.

**Prerequisites:**
- Organization status: Active
- Your user role: Organization Admin
- Team member email addresses

**Execution:**

1. Navigate to Settings > Team. Select **Invite Team Member**.
2. Enter the team member's institutional email address.
3. Select their role from the dropdown: Organization Manager, Team Member, Billing Admin, or Read-Only. A brand-new invite can't be sent as Organization Admin — that role can only be handed to an existing team member using **Transfer Admin**, which requires your password to confirm. Role definitions and capability matrices appear in [Roles & Permissions](/organizations/roles-permissions).
4. Select **Send Invite**. The system generates a unique invitation token with 7-day validity.
5. The system sends an invitation email to the specified address containing an acceptance link.
6. The invited team member clicks the link, reviews the invitation details, and enters a verification code sent to their email to confirm it's really them. If they don't already have a password on the platform, they set one here. They accept the Service Agreement and the role.

**Verification:** Team member appears in your organization roster with Accepted status. They log in successfully. Their role-based permissions restrict access and actions according to the role definition matrix.

## Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

**Objective:** Record your organization's agreement to the Business Associate Agreement.

**Prerequisites:**
- Your user role: Organization Admin

**Execution:**

1. Navigate to Settings > HIPAA Compliance.
2. Review the BAA document displayed on the page.
3. Select the checkbox confirming you have read and agree to the terms on behalf of your organization, and that you are authorized to accept it.
4. Select **Confirm agreement**.

**Verification:** The page displays the BAA as accepted. The action is recorded in the audit log.

---

**Note:** BAA acceptance is separate from organization registration and is not required to create an account or use the platform.
