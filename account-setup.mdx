Your Jaspari Studies account belongs to your organization as a whole. Once the organization is approved, you can invite colleagues, assign roles, and start designing studies.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup) and complete the organization application with your organization's details and contact information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Jaspari reviews your application before you can log in. You'll be notified once it's approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and invite your team">
    Once approved, log in and start inviting team members. See [Roles & Permissions](/organizations/roles-permissions) for the five roles available and what each one grants.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Accepting Jaspari's Business Associate Agreement is available from organization settings. This is optional and separate from launching a study.
</Note>

## Organizations that collaborate

Every organization on Jaspari Studies is the same kind of account, whether it's a clinic, a sponsor, a CRO, or a multi-site network. What differs is whether it owns its studies outright or collaborates on studies another organization owns, and at what tier.

Some organizations run entirely on their own: one organization, one team, its own studies and sites. Others work as part of a larger arrangement, such as an individual clinic collaborating on studies a sponsor or CRO owns, or several separately-run clinics each collaborating on studies a central organization owns. See [Collaboration](/organizations/collaboration) for how that works.
