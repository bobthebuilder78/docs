The Studies Platform does not have a procurement cycle or specialized onboarding cycle. Once your organization is approved, you build your study protocol, set a geographic scope, include goals, and launch on your own timeline.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your organization">
    Go to [studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup) and set up your organization. See [Account Setup](/account-setup) for what's needed to get approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your eligibility">
    Build your eligibility profile from conditions, medications, and quantitative or qualitative criteria. Listing several medications or conditions as ordinary rules means an interested volunteer qualifies with any one of them unless grouping is applied. Use grouping when you need to require more than one at once. See [Eligibility Parameters](/studies/eligibility-parameters).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a study format and add sites">
    Pick site-based, virtual, or hybrid. If your study uses physical sites, attach them now. You'll need to set that site's operating days and hours for appointments, available slots per period, and clinic closure dates. See [Study Formats](/studies/study-formats).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your geographic scope">
    Scope recruitment to a radius around a site, a list of ZIP codes, one or more states, or nationwide. Currently, only regions within the United States are available. See [Geographic Scope](/studies/geographic-scope).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your enrollment goal and launch">
    Enter your enrollment goal and target completion date, then launch. See [Setup & Launch](/studies/setup-and-launch) for what has to be true before a study can go live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pause your study at any time.">
    Your study may be manually paused at any time. Alternatively, if your goals are reached, your recruitment pauses, although your study stays active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Most study fields stay editable while a study is in draft, active, or paused. A small set of fields (name, study type, delivery model, phase, protocol number) lock the moment you launch. Confirm those before you go live.
</Tip>
