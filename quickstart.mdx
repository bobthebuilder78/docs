## Step 1: Register your organization

Navigate to [studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup). Registration runs in three steps.

Enter your name, email, phone number, and password, and accept the Service Agreement. Select **Continue**.

Enter the verification code sent to your email.

Enter your organization name, select organization type (clinic, sponsor, CRO, academic institution, government agency, or other), and enter your organization website.

Your organization enters Pending Review status. The platform emails you once it's approved. You can log in as soon as it is.

## Step 2: Invite team members

Once your organization is approved and Active, invite team members from Settings > Team. 

Each team member receives one of five roles: Organization Admin (full access), Organization Manager (manage studies and sites), Team Member (work leads), Billing Admin (invoices and payments), or Read-Only (view only). See [Roles & Permissions](/organizations/roles-permissions) for capability details.

Invitations expire after 7 days if not accepted.

## Step 3: Create and launch your first study

Navigate to Studies and select **Create Study**. Enter the study name and short description (used in volunteer outreach SMS).

You are redirected to the Setup wizard. Configure these required sections:

**Basic Information:** Protocol title, study type, delivery model (site-based, virtual, or hybrid), study phase, protocol number, and NCT number. These fields lock once you launch and cannot be changed.

**Eligibility Parameters:** Add conditions, medications, and structured rules (age, demographics, etc.). A volunteer must match your criteria to proceed.

**Geographic Scope:** Specify recruitment area (ZIP radius, multiple ZIP codes, state list, or nationwide).

**Sites:** For site-based and hybrid studies, assign at least one site with scheduling enabled.

**Recruitment Goals:** Set an enrollment target and target completion date.

**Launch:** Select **Launch Study** to move the study to Active status. Volunteer matching begins immediately.

Once launched:
- Identity fields (name, study type, delivery model, phase, protocol number) lock permanently
- Eligibility criteria, geographic scope, sites, and recruitment goals remain editable
- Most settings stay editable through Active and Paused statuses

See [Studies Overview](/studies/overview) for the complete status lifecycle.
