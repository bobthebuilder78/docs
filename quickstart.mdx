---
title: "Quickstart: Launch Your First Jaspari Study"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Sign up, create a study, add eligibility and geographic criteria, set enrollment goals, and launch. No sales cycle or implementation team needed."
---

You can go from signup to launched study in an afternoon. Jaspari Studies is self-service: there is no procurement cycle, no onboarding team, and no implementation project. Once your organization is approved and your BAA is executed, you build eligibility criteria, set geographic scope, define enrollment goals, and launch on your own timeline.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Visit [https://studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup) and create your organization account. You will be notified once your organization is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your organization">
    Go to [studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup) and set up your organization. See [Account Setup](/account-setup) for what's needed to get approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add eligibility parameters">
    Build inclusion and exclusion criteria using 4,000+ conditions, medications, and numeric criteria. Use boolean logic (for example, (A and B) or (A and not Y)) to match your protocol precisely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set geographic scope">
    Define where you want to recruit. Start with a ZIP radius, expand to statewide, or go nationwide. You can set scope per study.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set per-site enrollment goals">
    Enter enrollment targets for each site. The platform auto-routes volunteers to under-target sites so no single site falls behind.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Activate your study to begin recruiting pre-qualified participants. Monitor progress in real time and adjust scope or goals as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your eligibility">
    Build your eligibility profile from conditions, medications, and numeric criteria. Listing several medications or conditions as ordinary rules means a volunteer qualifies with any one of them. Use grouping when you need to require more than one at once. See [Eligibility Parameters](/studies/eligibility-parameters).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your geographic scope">
    Scope recruitment to a ZIP radius, a list of ZIP codes, statewide, or nationwide. See [Geographic Scope](/studies/geographic-scope).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a study format and add sites">
    Pick site-based, virtual, hybrid, or survey. If your study uses physical sites, attach them now. See [Study Formats](/studies/study-formats).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your enrollment goal and launch">
    Enter your enrollment goal and target completion date, complete any required compliance steps, and launch. See [Setup & Launch](/studies/setup-and-launch) for what has to be true before a study can go live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) must be executed at the organization level before you begin processing participant data. This single BAA covers all studies and users in your organization.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Every organization receives a dedicated liaison. If you need help, reach out to [support@jaspari.com](mailto:support@jaspari.com). Support is available 6 days per week.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Most study fields stay editable while a study is in draft, active, or paused. A small set of fields (name, study type, delivery model, phase, protocol number) lock the moment you launch. Confirm those before you go live.
</Tip>
