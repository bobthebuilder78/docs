Your organization maintains a prepaid wallet balance. Funds in your wallet are applied automatically to your invoices — see [Billing Cycles](/billing/billing-cycles) for exactly how wallet and card charges are combined on each invoice.

## Adding funds yourself

You can deposit funds to your wallet at any time from Settings > Billing, using a saved card or a new one. Deposited funds stay in your wallet indefinitely until they're applied to an invoice — they never expire.

## Credits Jaspari adds to your account

Jaspari staff can add funds to your wallet directly, without any card charge on your end. This is how a promotional credit is issued, and it's also used to correct a wallet mistake (for example, a deposit that landed against the wrong organization).

A credit like this shows up in your wallet transaction history the same way a deposit does, with a note explaining why it was added, and it's applied automatically to your next invoice(s) the same way any other wallet balance is — there's no separate "promo balance" to track or redeem.

<Note>
Jaspari has no automatic refund path. A credit added to your wallet is always a deliberate action by Jaspari staff, never an automatic response to a charge.
</Note>

## Monitoring your balance

Your wallet balance and full transaction history — deposits, invoice applications, and any credits added by Jaspari — are available in Settings > Billing, alongside your invoices and payment methods.
