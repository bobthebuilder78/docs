If your organization maintains a prepaid wallet balance, funds in your wallet are applied automatically to your invoices — see [Billing Cycles](/billing/billing-cycles) for exactly how wallet and card charges are combined on each invoice.

## Adding funds yourself

You can deposit funds to your wallet at any time from Settings > Billing, using a saved card or a new one. Deposited funds stay in your wallet indefinitely until they're applied to an invoice — they never expire.

## Funds added by your Jaspari

You may have funds added to your wallet directly by your platform's representative. This is how a promotional credit is issued. This may be used to issue promotional credits or correct a wallet mistake (for example, if you qualify for a platform-wide grant or you belong to mulitple organizations and forget to switch to the correct workspace before making a deposit).

A credit like this shows up in your wallet transaction history the same way a deposit does, with a note explaining why it was added, and it's applied automatically to your next invoice(s) the same way any other wallet balance.

<Note>
You may be prevented from making a wallet deposit if another teammate is already in the process of doing so.
</Note>

## Monitoring your balance

Your wallet balance and full transaction history (deposits, invoice applications, and any credits added by your platform's represenative) are available in Settings > Billing, alongside your invoices and payment methods.
