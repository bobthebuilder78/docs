Your organization has one wallet: a prepaid balance you can add funds to at any time. It works alongside invoices, not instead of them.

## How billing actually works

Charges (a volunteer accepting an offer, a booked visit) accumulate as they happen. On your organization's billing schedule — weekly, biweekly, or monthly, depending on your payment history — everything unbilled is swept into one invoice and charged immediately.

When an invoice is due, your wallet is checked first:

- If your wallet balance covers the full invoice, it's paid from the wallet with no card charge at all.
- If your wallet covers only part of it, that part is deducted and your card on file is charged for the remainder.
- If your wallet is empty, your card is charged for the full amount.

## Adding funds

You can deposit funds to your wallet at any time from your billing settings, using a saved card or a new one. Deposited funds don't expire and stay in your wallet until they're applied to an invoice.

## If a payment fails

An unpaid invoice doesn't suspend your organization or lock you out of the platform. Instead, it places a hold on new volunteer matching for the affected studies until the invoice is paid — you keep full access to log in and settle the balance. The platform retries a failed card automatically for several business days before the hold takes effect.

## Monitoring your balance

Your current wallet balance and transaction history are available in your organization's billing settings, alongside your invoices and payment methods.
