Jaspari Studies uses an organization wallet to manage all billing. Instead of invoicing per study, you add funds to your wallet as credits. The platform draws from those credits as your studies run. This keeps billing simple, transparent, and easy to forecast across multiple protocols and sites.

## How the Wallet Works

Every organization has a single wallet. When you launch a study, the platform reserves or draws from your wallet balance based on the study pricing formula. If you run multiple studies at once, credits are consumed in the order they were added, with promotional and refund credits typically applied before prepaid balance according to platform policy. You can add funds at any time, and any unused credits remain available for future studies.

## Credit Types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Prepaid Balance" icon="credit-card">
    Funds you add directly to your wallet through standard payment methods. These credits are available immediately and do not expire unless your organization account is closed.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Promotional Credit" icon="gift" href="/billing/promotional-credit">
    Credits earned through platform promotions, such as the Q4 2026 new-organization offer. These may carry expiration dates and usage conditions. See the [promotional credit page](/billing/promotional-credit) for current offers.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Refund Credit" icon="rotate-ccw">
    Credits issued when a study is paused or closed early and eligible funds are returned to your wallet. Refund credits are typically applied before prepaid balance.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Monitoring Balance and Expiration

You can view your current wallet balance, recent transactions, and any upcoming credit expirations in the billing section of your organization settings. The dashboard shows a breakdown by credit type so you can see how much is prepaid, promotional, or refunded. Review this regularly, especially if you are running multiple studies or working with time-limited promotional credits.

<Note>
Check expiration dates on promotional credits carefully. Expired credits are removed from your wallet automatically and cannot be reinstated.
</Note>
