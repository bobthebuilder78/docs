Your organization maintains a prepaid wallet balance. You can deposit funds at any time to apply toward future invoices.

## How billing actually works

Charges (a volunteer accepting an offer, a booked visit) accumulate as they happen. On your organization's billing schedule, which runs weekly, biweekly, or monthly depending on your payment history, everything unbilled is swept into one invoice and charged immediately.

When an invoice is due, the wallet is applied first:

- If the wallet balance covers the full invoice, it settles completely without a card charge.
- If the wallet covers only part of it, the remainder is charged to your card on file.
- If the wallet is empty, the card is charged for the full amount.

## Adding funds

You can deposit funds to your wallet at any time from your billing settings, using a saved card or a new one. Deposited funds stay in your wallet indefinitely until they're applied to an invoice.

## If a payment fails

An unpaid invoice places a hold on new volunteer matching until the invoice is paid. Leads already in the pipeline continue processing. You retain full access to your account and can pay the balance at any time. The platform retries a failed payment once per business day. A hold takes effect after the second failed attempt and remains in place until the balance is paid, even if retries continue for up to five business days.

## Monitoring your balance

Your wallet balance and transaction history are available in organization billing settings, along with invoices and payment methods.
