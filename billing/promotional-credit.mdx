Jaspari Studies is offering a limited-time credit for new organizations that start recruiting in the United States during Q4 2026. If your organization is new to the platform and you launch a qualifying US study, you can earn \$2,500 in wallet credit by reaching the required enrollment milestone before the quarter ends.

## Who Qualifies

This promotion is open to new organizations that sign up for Jaspari Studies and are approved to use the platform. Only studies conducted in the United States are eligible. Existing organizations and non-US studies do not qualify for this credit.

## How to Earn

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create your organization at [https://studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup) and complete the approval process. Once approved, you can access the platform immediately with no onboarding or sales cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch a qualifying US study">
    Build and launch a study that targets participants in the United States. Configure your eligibility parameters, geographic scope, and enrollment goals as you normally would.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach the enrollment milestone before end of Q4 2026">
    Hit the required enrollment target for your study before December 31, 2026. The platform tracks enrollment automatically and will notify you when the milestone is reached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credit posts to your wallet">
    Once the milestone is verified, the \$2,500 credit is added to your organization wallet automatically. You can use it toward any future study charges.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Terms

- One promotional credit per organization. Even if you launch multiple qualifying studies, the credit is awarded only once.
- The credit expires according to standard wallet policy. Check your billing dashboard for the exact expiration date after the credit is posted.
- US studies only. Studies with any non-US targeting are not eligible.
- The platform reserves the right to verify enrollment data before posting credit.
- This offer cannot be combined with other new-organization promotions unless explicitly stated.

<Warning>
  This offer ends at the close of Q4 2026 (December 31, 2026). Studies launched or milestones reached after this date will not qualify.
</Warning>
