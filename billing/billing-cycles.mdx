Charges (a volunteer accepting an offer, a booked visit) accumulate as they happen. They aren't billed individually. All charges within a billing period are included in one invoice on your organization's billing cycle and charged automatically.

## Your billing cadence

Every organization starts on a **weekly** billing cadence unless manually adjusted. After 3 consecutive on-time payments at your current cadence and continued use of the Studies Platform, you automatically move to longer billing cycles.

## How <u>each </u>invoice is paid

When an invoice comes due, your wallet balance is applied first:

- If your wallet balance covers the full invoice, it settles completely with no card charge.
- If your wallet covers only part of it, the remainder is charged to your default card.
- If your wallet is empty, your default card is charged for the full amount.

See [Payment Methods](/billing/payment-methods) for how your default card is chosen, and [Credits](/billing/credits) for how your wallet balance works.

## If a payment fails

- The platform retries a failed charge once per business day.
- After the second failed attempt, a **hold** takes effect: new volunteer matching pauses for your organization's studies until the balance is paid. Volunteers already in your pipeline keep moving, and your team keeps full access to the platform.
- Automatic retries continue for up to 5 business days from the original due date. After that, the platform stops retrying automatically, but the hold remains in place, and the invoice remains open, until the balance is paid.
- You can clear a hold at any time by paying the invoice directly or adding funds to your wallet; studies resume normal processing automatically once it's paid.

<Tip>
A payment hold never suspends accounts, blocks login, or affects leads already in progress.
</Tip>
