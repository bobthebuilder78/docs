Each study gets its own price per lead, calculated from a base rate plus two surcharges: how narrow your eligibility criteria make your pool, and how tightly you've scoped your recruitment area.

## What you're actually billed for

There are two billable events:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A volunteer accepts your study" icon="user-check">
    You're charged the study's price per lead the moment a volunteer accepts an offer to join your study. This is the core charge.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A visit gets booked" icon="calendar-check">
    If scheduling is turned on for a site and Jaspari books the visit, a separate flat scheduling fee is charged per booked appointment. This is independent of the per-lead price.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A volunteer who accepts but never books a visit is still billed as a lead, with no scheduling fee attached.

## How the per-lead price is calculated

The per-lead price is:

**floor + eligibility-criteria surcharge + geography surcharge**

- **Floor**: a base rate.
- **Eligibility-criteria surcharge**: based on how rare the combination of conditions, medications, and other inclusion/exclusion criteria you've selected is in the general population. Rarer combinations cost more to recruit for, so they carry a larger surcharge. Conditions, medications, and other criteria types are all priced the same way, regardless of which type of criterion it is.
- **Geography surcharge**: based on the population inside your chosen recruitment area. A smaller population, such as a tight ZIP radius, carries a larger surcharge than a large one such as statewide or nationwide coverage, since a smaller pool is harder to recruit from.

## You see a price, you're billed on it

While a study is in draft, the price estimate you see updates live as you change eligibility criteria or recruitment area, tracking the platform's current pricing formula. The moment you launch, that number locks in permanently for the study. From then on, only a deliberate edit to the study itself, meaning its criteria, its recruitment area, or its sites, recalculates and re-locks the price. Unrelated changes elsewhere on the platform, like a pricing-data refresh or a site becoming inactive, leave your locked-in price alone.

The one exception: if Jaspari has manually customized pricing for your specific study, the pricing screen tells you so explicitly, and your price may not match the standard formula.

## Pausing and resuming

You can pause and resume a study at any time. See [Launch, Pause, and Amend a Study](/studies/launching-pausing) for how pausing affects leads already in progress. If your calculated price would cross the platform's custom-quote threshold, the estimate is hidden in favor of "contact us for a custom quote," and the study is held from going live (or resuming from pause) until that's resolved.

<Tip>
The pricing estimate on your study's setup page is the same number, computed the same way, that you'll actually be charged. If Jaspari has customized your study's pricing, the page says so explicitly.
</Tip>
