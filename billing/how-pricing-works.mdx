---

Each study gets its own price, calculated from your specific eligibility criteria and geographic scope rather than a flat per-participant rate. This means your price reflects how difficult your specific cohort is to recruit, not an average across all studies on the platform.

Jaspari Studies uses a formula-driven price for every study. Instead of flat fees or bundled packages, your cost reflects the actual complexity of your recruitment: how specific your eligibility criteria are, how wide your geographic reach is, and which features you enable. There are no minimum spend requirements, no contract lock-ins, and no termination fees. You pay only for what you configure.

## Predicting spend before launch

Because pricing is calculated before you launch, you can review projected cost against your budget while the study is still in draft. Set a per-study spend limit to cap costs at a defined ceiling — recruitment pauses automatically once that limit is reached, so spend can't exceed what you approved.

## Pausing and resuming

You can pause and resume recruitment at any time without losing your study configuration. Enrollment goals and spend limits can be adjusted between pause and resume as your protocol evolves. See <a href="/studies/launching-pausing">Launch, Pause, and Complete a Study</a> for how pausing affects in-flight leads.

## Portfolio billing for research organizations

If your organization manages multiple studies, billing can be structured across your portfolio rather than tracked individually at the study level. Billing responsibility and study ownership are tracked separately, so a study's charges can resolve to your organization independently of which site or team actually operates it. Contact your account liaison to discuss portfolio-level billing arrangements.

<Tip>
If your eligibility criteria are unusually complex — for example, requirements beyond what the standard rule builder supports, described in <a href="/studies/eligibility-parameters">Eligibility Parameters</a> — contact your liaison before launching so pricing can be assessed accurately.
</Tip>

## No Minimums or Contracts

You can launch a study with any budget. There is no minimum spend, no required contract length, and no penalty for ending a study early. If you pause or close a study, you stop incurring charges for that study immediately. Unused credits remain in your wallet for future use.

## Where to See Your Estimate

As you configure a study, the platform displays a live price estimate on the study configuration screen. The estimate updates in real time when you change eligibility criteria, expand geographic scope, or toggle features. Review the estimate before you launch so you know exactly what the study will cost.
