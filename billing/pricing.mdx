Each study gets its own price per lead, calculated from a base rate plus two surcharges: how narrow your eligibility criteria make your pool, and how tightly you've scoped your recruitment area.

## What you're actually billed for

There are two billable events:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A volunteer accepts your study" icon="user-check">
    You're charged the study's price per lead the moment a volunteer accepts an offer to join your study. This is the core charge.
  </Card>

  <Card title="A visit gets booked" icon="calendar-check">
    If scheduling is turned on for a site and a visit is automatically booked, a separate flat scheduling fee is charged per booked appointment. This is independent of the per-lead price.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A volunteer who accepts but never books a visit is still billed as a lead, with no scheduling fee attached.

## Automatic pricing vs. tailored pricing

Every study is priced one of two ways:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Automatic (formula) pricing" icon="calculator">
    The default for every study. Your per-lead price is computed by the platform's pricing formula (base rate plus the eligibility and geography surcharges below) and recalculates automatically whenever something priced-relevant about the study changes — an edit to criteria or recruitment area, or a change to one of its sites. Nothing here is set by a person.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tailored (enterprise) pricing" icon="handshake">
    For a study flagged as enterprise, either from the start or later, Jaspari sets a fixed per-lead price directly instead of running the formula. Once that price is set, it's permanent for the study — the formula never runs for it again, even if criteria or recruitment area change later. If no price has been set yet for a study in this state, the study simply isn't chargeable until one is.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If your study has a tailored price, the pricing screen tells you so explicitly rather than showing the formula breakdown, and your number may not match what the standard formula would otherwise produce.

## How the automatic (formula) price is calculated

The per-lead price is made up of a base rate plus two surcharges:

- **Base rate**: a starting price for every study.
- **Eligibility-criteria surcharge**: based on how rare the combination of conditions, medications, and other inclusion or exclusion criteria you've selected is in the general population. Rarer combinations cost more to recruit for, so they carry a larger surcharge.
- **Geography surcharge**: based on the population inside your chosen recruitment area. A smaller population, such as a tight ZIP radius, carries a larger surcharge than a large one such as statewide or nationwide coverage, since a smaller pool is harder to recruit from.

## You see a price, you're billed on it

While a study is in draft, the price estimate you see updates live as you change eligibility criteria or recruitment area, tracking the platform's current pricing formula. The moment you launch, that number locks in permanently for the study. From then on, the price recalculates only when something about the study itself changes: an edit to its criteria or recruitment area, or a change to one of its sites (including a site's address or a site becoming inactive). A platform-wide pricing update on its own does not touch a study's already-locked price.

## Pausing and resuming

You can pause and resume a study at any time. See [Launch, Pause, and Amend a Study](/studies/launching-pausing) for how pausing affects leads already in progress. If your calculated price would cross the platform's custom-quote threshold, the estimate is hidden in favor of "contact us for a custom quote," and the study cannot go live or resume from pause until the study is priced under the threshold or a tailored price is set.

<Tip>
The pricing estimate on your study's setup page is the same number, computed the same way, that you'll actually be charged. If your study has a tailored price, the page says so explicitly.
</Tip>
