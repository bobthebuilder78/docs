Jaspari Studies includes an IRB Library with pre-made recruitment materials designed to simplify IRB review. Instead of drafting recruitment content from scratch, you can select templates that have been structured for common study types and therapeutic areas, then customize them for your protocol.

## What's Included

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Recruitment Copy" icon="file-text">
    Pre-written recruitment language for ads, landing pages, and email outreach. Copy is structured to meet common IRB expectations for accuracy, tone, and required disclosures.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Imagery" icon="image">
    Stock imagery and graphic templates appropriate for clinical research recruitment. Materials are selected to avoid misleading or coercive visual cues.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Channel Descriptions" icon="globe">
    Descriptions of where and how recruitment will occur (social media, search, patient communities, site databases). These help IRBs understand your outreach scope.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Consent Language Templates" icon="clipboard">
    Draft language for informed consent documents related to recruitment-specific data collection and contact methods.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Use It

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select templates for your study">
    Browse the IRB Library by therapeutic area and study format. Choose the recruitment copy, imagery, and channel descriptions that match your protocol.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize for your protocol">
    Edit the templates to include your study-specific details: site names, contact information, inclusion and exclusion criteria, and any protocol-specific risks or benefits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bundle for your IRB submission">
    Download the complete set of materials as a single package. Submit alongside your protocol and consent documents to streamline IRB review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Using library materials often accelerates IRB approval because the content is pre-structured around common IRB concerns. Many organizations find that reviewers ask fewer questions when the recruitment plan follows a familiar format.
</Tip>
