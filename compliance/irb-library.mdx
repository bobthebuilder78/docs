Organization Admins can access the IRB Library from organization settings. It documents the generic
placeholders, imagery categories, and recruiting channels used across recruitment content, for use when
preparing materials for IRB review.

<Note>
  Requirements vary by IRB. Confirm directly with the study's IRB of record what needs review before
  launching. Any alterations to this library only apply to new studies, and users are notified when that
  happens. To use your own materials instead, email them along with your organization's sharing code to
  irb@jaspari.com before launching your studies.
</Note>

## Text placeholders

Recruitment content uses a fixed set of placeholders rather than free text. These cover the study name, condition name, and site location as they appear on the study record; the eligibility criteria bullets, pulled from the study's eligibility summary; and compensation phrasing, which is optional and configured in [Setup](/studies/setup-and-launch).

## Imagery categories

Photos used in recruitment materials are drawn from basic  five categories: outdoor lifestyle, home lifestyle, neutral/cartoonish (no people, icon-only), age-matched to the study's eligible range, and group or family shots. Imagery is never
shown depicting a clinical setting, medication use, a procedure, or any particular reaction to the study
itself. The photo pool is added to on an ongoing basis, and each new photo is checked against its
category's description before use.

## Recruiting channels

The library also documents every channel recruitment materials can run through.

## Downloading examples

Each text placeholder and imagery category has an example file that can be downloaded from the library.
