Separate from what a person can *access* (their role, and their organization's standing on a shared
study), volunteer data visibility controls how much of a volunteer or lead's record they can actually
*see* once they reach it.

## The three levels

- **Full** — everything, no restriction.
- **Anonymized** — everything except direct identifiers: first name, last name, phone, email, and full
  date of birth, plus status notes and Local details. Age still shows; the volunteer is identified by their
  public ID instead of their name.
- **None** — no volunteer or lead data at all. Lists appear empty and the volunteer detail page is
  unavailable.

This applies everywhere volunteer or lead data appears: pipeline views, the leads list, the screening
queue, scheduled visits, appointments and calendars, volunteer detail pages, status history, and audit log
entries about a volunteer.

At **Anonymized**, viewing is unaffected for everything else, but adding or editing a status note, editing
Local details, changing a name/phone/email/date of birth, and adding a lead manually are all blocked, since
those all involve entering or editing a direct identifier.

## Who sets it

Each person has a default visibility level tied to their role, set automatically when they join and
adjustable by an Organization Admin from Team settings. An Organization Admin can also raise or lower a
specific teammate's visibility for an individual study.

Anyone, including an Organization Admin, can lower their own visibility at any time. Nobody can raise
their own visibility — raising it back up always requires an Organization Admin to do it on that person's
behalf.

For the full permissions model this sits alongside, see [Roles &
Permissions](https://studies-guides.jaspari.com/organizations/roles-permissions#volunteer-data-visibility).
