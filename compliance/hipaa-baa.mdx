Organization Admins can review and accept a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) from organization settings.

<Note>
  This page intentionally stays brief. Details on what the BAA covers, its execution process, and Jaspari's specific data-protection practices aren't confirmed in our current documentation — please check with your Jaspari contact for specifics before relying on this page for compliance planning.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Accepting the BAA is **not** required to launch or resume a study. Don't assume otherwise if you're timing a launch against your compliance review.
</Warning>
