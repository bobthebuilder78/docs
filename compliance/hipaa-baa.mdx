Organization Admins can review and accept a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) from organization settings.

If your organization is a covered entity under HIPAA, a BAA is required by federal regulations before sharing protected health information with a vendor. Accepting it is how that requirement gets satisfied on this platform.

<Note>
  This page intentionally stays brief. For details about BAA coverage, execution, and data-protection practices, contact your platform representative before relying on this page for compliance planning.
</Note>

<Warning>
  You can launch or resume a study freely regardless of your BAA acceptance status. Time your compliance review on its own schedule.
</Warning>
