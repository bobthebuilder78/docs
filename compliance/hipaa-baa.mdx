Jaspari Studies is built to meet HIPAA requirements for clinical research recruitment. Every organization executes a single Business Associate Agreement (BAA) that covers all studies, users, and participants under that organization. This page explains what the BAA covers, how Jaspari protects data, and how to execute your agreement.

## What the BAA Covers

The BAA is executed at the organization level, so one agreement covers every study and every user under your organization. This means you do not need to sign a separate BAA for each protocol or site. The agreement defines how Jaspari handles protected health information (PHI) on your behalf, including obligations for data security, breach notification, and permitted uses and disclosures. Once executed, the BAA remains in effect for all current and future studies under that organization.

## Data Protection Practices

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Encryption in Transit and at Rest" icon="lock">
    All data is encrypted when transmitted over the network and when stored on our systems. This includes participant contact information, eligibility responses, and study metadata.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Scoped Access Controls" icon="shield">
    Permissions are scoped per user, role, study, and site. A user can only access the records and actions their role explicitly allows, and administrators can revoke access at any time.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Audit Logging" icon="file-text" href="/compliance/audit-log">
    Every login, record view, permission change, and study action is captured in a full timestamped audit log. This supports regulatory reviews, sponsor audits, and internal oversight.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Vendor Review" icon="check-circle">
    Jaspari reviews subprocessors and vendors against HIPAA security requirements. Organizations can request a current vendor list and security assessment from their liaison at any time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Executing Your BAA

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request via signup">
    Start by signing up at [studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup). During the approval process, you will be prompted to request a BAA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review with your liaison">
    Your dedicated Jaspari liaison will send the BAA draft and walk through any questions with your legal or compliance team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute">
    Sign the agreement electronically or via countersignature, depending on your organization's preference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Once executed, your organization is cleared to build and launch studies. The BAA applies to all users and studies under your organization automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The executed BAA is a prerequisite to launching a study. You can set up eligibility parameters, geographic scope, and enrollment goals before the BAA is finalized, but recruitment cannot go live until the agreement is in place.
</Note>
