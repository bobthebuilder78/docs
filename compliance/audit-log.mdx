An audit log tracks activity on your organization's account, available to Organization Admins from organization settings. Logged activity includes team changes, study status changes, and billing events. Entries are retained indefinitely.

## Who can see it

Access to the audit log is limited to Organization Admins.

## Downloading the log

From the Audit Log page, select **Download** to export the log as a CSV file. This is useful for sharing activity records with a sponsor or during a regulatory review.
