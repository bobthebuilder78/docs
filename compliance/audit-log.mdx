Jaspari Studies keeps an audit log of activity on your organization's account, available to Organization Admins from organization settings.

<Note>
  This page intentionally stays brief. Details like exactly which events are captured, export options, and retention periods aren't confirmed in our current documentation — if you need specifics for a sponsor or regulatory review, please check with your Jaspari contact before relying on this page for compliance planning.
</Note>

## Who can see it

Access to the audit log is limited to Organization Admins.
