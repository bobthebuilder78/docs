Jaspari Studies records a full timestamped audit trail of every action taken on the platform. This log supports 21 CFR Part 11 compliance, sponsor audits, and internal reviews by capturing who did what, and when.

## What Gets Logged

The audit log captures the following events automatically:

- **Logins** (successful and failed attempts)
- **Record views** (participant profiles, eligibility responses, enrollment data)
- **Permission changes** (role assignments, access grants, and revocations)
- **Study sharing** (inviting collaborators, sharing with sponsors or CROs)
- **Parameter and geo changes** (eligibility criteria edits, geographic scope adjustments)
- **Launches and pauses** (when a study goes live or is paused)
- **Amendments** (protocol changes that affect recruitment scope)

## Exporting for Audits

You can export a time-ranged audit log from your organization settings. Select a start and end date, filter by event type or user if needed, and download the report. The export includes timestamps, user identifiers, action types, and affected records. Provide this file directly to sponsors, auditors, or your compliance team.

## Retention

Audit logs are retained for the life of your organization account plus a standard post-termination period required for regulatory compliance. If you need a specific retention schedule or extended archive for a sponsor requirement, contact your liaison.

<Tip>
  Pull an audit log export before a sponsor review or inspection to have a complete, timestamped record ready. This can reduce follow-up questions and demonstrate control over your recruitment process.
</Tip>
