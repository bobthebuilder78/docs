Jaspari Studies supports every major research format used in the United States. The format you choose determines how volunteers are matched to sites, what geographic rules apply, and how recruitment messaging is framed. Pick the format during study setup; you can amend it later if your protocol changes.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Standard In-Person">
    **Use case:** Volunteers must visit a physical site for screening, dosing, assessments, or all visits.

    **Matching behavior:** The platform matches by both eligibility and geography, then assigns the volunteer to the nearest participating site that is within the site's defined catchment area and behind its enrollment target.

    **Typical protocol shape:** Site-based visits, on-site labs, in-clinic assessments. Recruitment messaging emphasizes location and convenience.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Hybrid">
    **Use case:** A mix of in-person and remote activities. Some visits happen on-site, others by phone or video.

    **Matching behavior:** Similar to standard in-person: the platform uses site-level geography and enrollment targets to route volunteers. The hybrid label is surfaced in recruitment materials so volunteers understand the expected commitment before they express interest.

    **Typical protocol shape:** Baseline and endpoint on-site, interim follow-ups remote. Useful for reducing travel burden while retaining physical assessments.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Fully Virtual / Decentralized">
    **Use case:** No physical site visits required. All activities, including consent, screening, and data collection, happen remotely.

    **Matching behavior:** The platform matches by eligibility and recruitment area (statewide or nationwide), but does not assign volunteers to a specific site. Instead, the volunteer is connected to the study's central coordination team or to the nearest virtual-capable site for administrative purposes.

    **Typical protocol shape:** eConsent, telehealth visits, home-based assessments, direct-to-patient shipping. Geography is used for time-zone and licensing alignment rather than travel distance.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Survey">
    **Use case:** Observational or registry studies that require only questionnaire completion, often with no clinical intervention.

    **Matching behavior:** The platform screens for eligibility and routes the volunteer to the survey instrument. Site assignment is optional; if sites are involved, they are selected by administrative load rather than proximity.

    **Typical protocol shape:** Online questionnaires, occasional follow-up surveys, minimal or no clinical contact. Fastest to launch and often the widest geographic scope.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Changing Format Mid-Study

If your protocol is amended, you can switch the study format from your study settings. The platform updates matching behavior and recruitment messaging automatically. Volunteers who were already matched under the prior format remain in their current assignment unless you manually reassign them.
