Every study has a delivery model: **site-based**, **virtual**, or **hybrid**. You choose it when you create the study, and it locks permanently the moment the study leaves draft — it can't be changed afterward.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Site-Based">
    The default. Volunteers are expected to visit a physical site.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Virtual">
    No physical site visits. A virtual study is never assigned any sites.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Hybrid">
    A mix of in-person and remote activity. For nearly every practical purpose, a hybrid study behaves the same as a site-based one — for example, volunteers still see a Sites section on the study's volunteer-facing page, unlike a virtual study.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What the format actually controls

It's narrower than the three labels might suggest:

- **Whether volunteers see a Sites section** on your study's page. Hidden only for virtual studies — hybrid is treated the same as site-based here.
- **A setup-wizard checklist indicator** showing whether you still need to assign a site. This is just a visual reminder and doesn't by itself block you from launching.

**What actually determines whether a lead gets scheduled through a site has nothing to do with the format label.** It comes down to whether you've assigned at least one site to the study:

- If you have assigned sites, leads route to one of them (and can be scheduled there, once that site has scheduling turned on and working).
- If you haven't assigned any sites — whether because the study is virtual by design, or because it's a site-based/hybrid study that just hasn't had a site attached yet — leads are created without a site and aren't automatically scheduled. You (or the volunteer, if more than one site is available) can assign or choose a site later.

In other words: a site-based study with no sites yet behaves identically, in practice, to a virtual study — until you add one.

## Setting it up

Pick the delivery model when you create your study, on the Basic Information step. Since it locks the moment you launch, confirm it's right before you go live.
