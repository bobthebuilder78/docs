A study on Jaspari Studies is the single container that powers your recruitment campaign. It bundles who you need (eligibility), where you need them (geography), and how many you need (enrollment goals) into a matching engine that surfaces pre-qualified volunteers to your sites. Once your organization is approved, you can create, configure, and launch studies without any onboarding calls or implementation team.

## Anatomy of a Study

Every study is built from four core components. Click a card to jump to the detailed guide.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Eligibility Parameters" icon="list-checks" href="/studies/eligibility-parameters">
    Define inclusion and exclusion criteria using 4,000+ conditions, medications, and numeric thresholds. Build complex logic with boolean operators.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Geographic Scope" icon="map-pin" href="/studies/geographic-scope">
    Set where volunteers must live or work, from a ZIP radius up to statewide or nationwide coverage. Control per site or study-wide.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enrollment Goals" icon="target" href="/studies/enrollment-goals">
    Assign per-site targets and let the platform auto-route volunteers to sites that are behind their goals.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Study Formats" icon="layout-template" href="/studies/study-formats">
    Choose standard in-person, hybrid, fully virtual/decentralized, or survey formats. Matching behavior adapts to the format you select.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Study Lifecycle

Most studies move through the same five stages. You control every transition from your dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Create a new study and name it. At this stage it is invisible to volunteers and you can iterate freely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Add eligibility parameters, set geographic scope, assign enrollment targets, and pick a study format. Invite collaborating sites if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    When you are ready, activate the study. Jaspari Studies begins identifying and screening volunteers against your criteria immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor">
    Track screened-in counts, site-level progress, and spend in real time. Amend parameters or adjust scope without pausing if your protocol allows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Amend or Pause">
    Update criteria, widen geography, or reallocate targets as the study evolves. Pause recruitment at any time if enrollment is complete or the protocol changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
