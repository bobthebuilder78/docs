A study is the container that powers your recruitment campaign. It bundles who you need (eligibility criteria), where you need them (geography), and how you're billed and capped (pricing and lead limits) into a matching engine that surfaces volunteers to your sites.

## Study lifecycle

A study moves through a fixed set of statuses:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Create a new study and configure it. At this stage it stays invisible to volunteers and most fields are freely editable. A draft moves forward to active only, with no path back once launched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Active">
    Once launched, volunteer matching against your criteria begins automatically. Some fields (name, study type, delivery model, phase, and protocol number) lock permanently and cannot be changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paused">
    Pause recruitment at any time. A paused study can be resumed back to active, or ended.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ended or Completed">
    Terminal and permanent. No changes can be made after reaching these states. Contact your platform representative if corrections are needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
