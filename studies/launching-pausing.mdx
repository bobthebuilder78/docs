For what has to be true before a study can launch, and what locks once it does, see [Setup & Launch](/studies/setup-and-launch).

## Launching

Once your study configuration is complete, select **Launch** in the study controls. Jaspari begins matching volunteers against your criteria immediately.

<Note>
  Accepting Jaspari's Business Associate Agreement is not required to launch a study.
</Note>

## Pausing a study

Pause recruitment at any time from the study dashboard. When a study is paused:

- No new leads are matched or sent to sites.
- Your configuration and all historical data stay exactly as they were.
- Most fields remain editable, the same as while the study is active.

Pausing is a reasonable choice when a site needs time to catch up on existing leads, or a protocol change needs review before recruitment continues.

## Resuming a study

Open the study dashboard and resume it. Recruitment restarts with the same configuration — nothing needs reconfiguring unless you want to make a change.

<Note>
  If your organization has an outstanding billing hold, resuming won't restart matching until the hold clears. See [Wallet & Credits](/billing/wallet-credits). If your study's price has drifted over the platform's custom-quote threshold since it last ran, resuming is blocked until that's resolved.
</Note>

## Making changes while a study is active or paused

Most fields — eligibility rules, description, contact info, visibility settings, compensation, recruitment dates and area — stay editable through both active and paused. A small set of identity fields (name, study type, delivery model, phase, protocol number) lock permanently the moment a study first leaves draft. See [Setup & Launch](/studies/setup-and-launch) for the full breakdown.

<Warning>
  Saving eligibility rules replaces the full rule set, not just what you changed. A change applies to matching going forward — a volunteer whose eligibility form is already open when you save will be evaluated against whatever rules are live at the moment they submit, not a frozen copy from before your change.
</Warning>

## Ending a study

When enrollment is done and recruitment is no longer needed, move the study to ended or completed from the study dashboard. Both are final — nothing in the portal can move a study back out of either status.

<Tip>
  If you're not certain enrollment is truly final — for example, some sites still have leads mid-pipeline — pause rather than complete. A paused study can resume. A completed one can't.
</Tip>
