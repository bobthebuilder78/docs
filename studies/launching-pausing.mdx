Jaspari Studies lets you take a study live as soon as your configuration and compliance steps are complete. You can pause, resume, or amend an active study at any time, and you never pay termination fees or penalties for stopping recruitment.

For what has to be true before a study can launch, and what locks once it does, see [Setup & Launch](/studies/setup-and-launch).

## Pausing a study

Before volunteers begin matching, confirm that your study setup is finalized and your organization is approved.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review configuration">
    Open your study and verify the eligibility parameters, geographic scope, enrollment goals, and study format. Make sure the criteria reflect the current protocol.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm BAA is executed">
    Ensure your organization has a signed Business Associate Agreement on file. The BAA is executed at the organization level and covers all studies and users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Launch">
    Select **Launch** in the study controls. Once live, Jaspari begins identifying and screening volunteers against your criteria and routing pre-qualified participants to your sites.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Pause recruitment at any time from the study dashboard. When a study is paused:

- No new leads are matched or sent to sites.
- Your configuration and all historical data stay exactly as they were.
- Most fields remain editable, the same as while the study is active.

Pausing is a reasonable choice when a site needs time to catch up on existing leads, or a change to your protocol needs review before recruitment continues.

## Pausing and resuming Resuming a study

Open the study dashboard and resume it. Recruitment restarts from wherever it left off. Nothing needs reconfiguring unless you want to make a change.

<Note>
  If your organization has an outstanding billing hold, resuming won't restart matching until the hold clears. See [Wallet & Credits](/billing/wallet-credits).
</Note>

## Making changes while a study is active or paused

You can pause an active study at any time to temporarily stop new volunteer screening and routing. Pausing does not affect participants already in the pipeline. When you are ready to resume, select **Resume** and recruitment continues with the same configuration.

<Note>
  There are no termination fees or penalties for pausing, stopping, or amending a study. Pricing is formula-driven per study with no minimum spend and no contract length.
</Note>

## Protocol amendments

Most fields, eligibility rules, description, contact info, visibility settings, compensation, recruitment dates and area, stay editable through both active and paused. A small set of identity fields (name, study type, delivery model, phase, protocol number) lock permanently the moment a study first leaves draft. See [Setup & Launch](/studies/setup-and-launch) for the full breakdown.

<Warning>
  A change to eligibility criteria applies to matching going forward. A volunteer already matched or screened under the prior criteria stays governed by whatever version was in effect at the time, which keeps an accurate record of what applied to each volunteer.
</Warning>

When enrollment is done and recruitment is no longer needed, move the study to ended or completed from the study dashboard. Both are final: nothing in the portal can move a study back out of either status. All records and audit history stay accessible in your organization's study history.

<Tip>
  If you're not certain enrollment is truly final, for example some sites still have leads mid-pipeline, pause rather than complete. A paused study can resume. A completed one can't.
</Tip>

When you amend an active study, the updated eligibility parameters, geographic scope, or enrollment goals apply going forward. Jaspari maintains a full version history so prior criteria remain auditable per volunteer. This supports sponsor audits and regulatory review, including 21 CFR Part 11 requirements.

If you need help validating an amendment before pushing it live, contact your dedicated liaison or reach out to [support@jaspari.com](mailto:support@jaspari.com).
