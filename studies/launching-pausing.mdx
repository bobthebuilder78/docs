For what has to be true before a study can launch, and what locks once it does, see [Setup & Launch](/studies/setup-and-launch).

## Launching

Once your study configuration is complete, select **Launch** in the study controls. Volunteer matching against your criteria begins immediately.


## Pausing a study

Pause recruitment at any time from the study dashboard. When a study is paused:

- Leads stop being matched and sent to sites.
- Your configuration and all historical data stay exactly as they were.
- Most fields remain editable, the same as while the study is active.

Pausing is a reasonable choice when a site needs time to catch up on existing leads, or a protocol change needs review before recruitment continues.

## Resuming a study

Open the study dashboard and resume it. Recruitment restarts with the same configuration, ready to go as-is unless you want to make a change.

<Note>
  If your organization has an outstanding billing hold, matching resumes once the hold clears. See [Wallet & Credits](/billing/wallet-credits). If your study's price has drifted over the platform's custom-quote threshold since it last ran, resolve that first before resuming.
</Note>

## Making changes while a study is active or paused

Most fields, including eligibility rules, description, contact info, visibility settings, compensation, and recruitment dates and area, stay editable through both active and paused. A small set of identity fields (name, study type, delivery model, phase, protocol number) lock permanently the moment a study first leaves draft. See [Setup & Launch](/studies/setup-and-launch) for the full breakdown.

<Warning>
  Saving eligibility rules replaces the full rule set as a complete swap. A change applies to matching going forward: a volunteer whose eligibility form is already open when you save gets evaluated against whatever rules are live at the moment they submit, current at that exact moment.
</Warning>

## Ending a study

When enrollment is done and recruitment is no longer needed, move the study to ended or completed from the study dashboard. Both are final, permanent statuses within the portal.

<Tip>
  If you're not certain enrollment is truly final, for example if some sites still have leads mid-pipeline, pause. A paused study stays resumable, while a completed one is final.
</Tip>
