title: "Set Enrollment Goals and Lead Limits"

---

A study has two separate things that sound similar but do different jobs: an enrollment goal, which is for your own tracking, and lead limits, which actually control the flow of leads. Both are set at the study level — there is no per-site version of either.

## Enrollment goal

Enter an enrollment goal and a target completion date on the study's Recruitment Goals tab. This is informational only. It doesn't route volunteers, doesn't pause anything, and doesn't change how leads are matched. It's there so you and your team have a number to measure progress against.

## Max leads and soft limit

These are the settings that actually gate how many leads a study receives.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Max leads" icon="circle-stop">
    A hard, permanent cap on how many leads the study can ever receive. Once a study reaches its max leads, it stops receiving new leads entirely — enforced at the moment a volunteer accepts an offer, so two volunteers can't both take the last open slot.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Soft limit" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    An earlier checkpoint. Once a study crosses its soft limit, you get an email that new leads are winding down — a couple already on the way may still arrive.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If you don't set a soft limit, it falls back to your max leads. Both are editable from the study's Recruitment Goals tab at any time the study isn't ended or completed, and changes take effect immediately.

<Note>
  If you set soft limit equal to max leads, you'll only get the "reached lead limit" notice — there's no separate "approaching" moment to report when the two are the same.
</Note>

## Adjusting limits mid-study

You can raise or lower max leads and soft limit at any time. Common reasons:

- Enrollment is going faster than expected and you want to stop new leads early.
- A sponsor or IRB amendment changes your target sample size.
- You want an earlier warning before you hit your cap, so you set a soft limit below it.

## Studies with more than one site

Max leads and soft limit apply to the whole study, not to any one site. There is no per-site enrollment target and no automatic routing of volunteers toward under-enrolled sites. If you need enrollment concentrated at a particular site, that's managed through which sites are assigned to the study and each site's own scheduling availability — see [Sites](/organizations/sites) — not through the enrollment goal or lead limits.

## Virtual studies

The same two settings apply: an enrollment goal for tracking, and max leads/soft limit for actual control. Since a virtual study has no sites, matching is based on recruitment area alone.
