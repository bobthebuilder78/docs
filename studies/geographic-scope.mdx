Jaspari Studies lets you define where volunteers must live or work before they are matched to your study. You can set geography at the study level or independently for each site, so recruitment spend is directed only where you need it.

## Scope Levels

Pick the level that fits your protocol and site footprint. You can change this at any time without creating a new study.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="ZIP Radius" icon="navigation">
    Draw a circle around a specific ZIP code and recruit only volunteers within that radius. Ideal for single-site studies with strict travel requirements or local community outreach.
  </Card>

  <Card title="County" icon="map">
    Expand to full county coverage when your site draws from a broader metro area. Useful when ZIP-level targeting is too narrow but statewide reach is unnecessary.
  </Card>

  <Card title="State" icon="landmark">
    Cover an entire state for multi-site networks or state-specific registries. Each site can claim its own statewide catchment, or you can run a single statewide campaign.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Nationwide" icon="globe">
    Recruit across the United States for fully virtual or decentralized studies, or for large networks with sites in many states. The platform routes volunteers to the nearest participating site automatically.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Multi-Site Studies

When a study includes more than one site, each site can have its own independent geographic scope. A volunteer who lives inside two overlapping catchment areas is routed to the site that needs enrollment most, based on your per-site targets and the auto-routing rules.

If you prefer, you can also set a single study-wide geography and let the platform assign volunteers to sites by proximity and enrollment progress.

## Adjusting Scope

You can widen or narrow geographic scope at any point during the study. Common reasons to adjust include:

- Opening a new site and expanding its catchment area
- Tightening radius after a protocol amendment limits travel
- Switching from statewide to nationwide for a virtual arm

Changes take effect immediately for new volunteer matches. Existing qualified volunteers are not removed unless your protocol requires re-evaluation.
