Every volunteer in your portal has exactly one status at a time, organized into nine groups shown as tabs on your Volunteers page. The Miscellaneous category is designed to accomodate volunteers who may not fit into a particular status. Custom notes may be attached to this volunteer, as with all volunteers.

## Updating a lead's status

Click into any lead and use the status dropdown to move them between statuses. This is for your own tracking. Updating a volunteer's status has no effect on whether or how new volunteers are sent to you. For example, if a volunteer randomizes into a study and has their status set to Randomized, it does not alter your enrollment goals. Use notes on the lead's history to record why a status changed, since there's no separate reason field beyond each group's Miscellaneous catch-all.

## Scheduled visits

When scheduling is enabled, several statuses in the Scheduled group update automatically:

- **Confirmation Pending**: a scheduling link has gone out and we're waiting for the volunteer to pick a time.
- **Confirmed Automatically**: the volunteer completed their booking through the automated system.
- **Confirmed by Staff**: your team confirmed the visit manually, a front-desk confirmation right before the appointment, or a visit your staff booked by hand.

Your team can also set a visit to **Confirmed by Staff** . For example, a front-desk confirmation right before the appointment, or a visit your staff booked by hand. This status is always set manually and never applied automatically.

If a site change (hours, capacity, time zone, or the site going inactive) makes an appointment that's still Confirmed by Jaspari no longer valid, that appointment is cancelled automatically and the volunteer is texted a cancellation notice.

There is a 3-hour grace period first, so a mistaken correction to the site settings will not automatically cancel appointments, except for appointments happening within the next 4 hours, which are resolved right away. A notice will be made available before you make any changes that may affect volunteers' appointments. Jaspari does not automatically rebook a cancelled visit, so your team will need to follow up directly to schedule a new time.

## Email notifications

From **Settings → Notifications**, you can control who on your team gets emailed and when, for these five types:

| Notification | Default | Who can receive it |
|---|---|---|
| New lead matched | On | Any team member with the toggle on |
| Maximum matched leads (covers both approaching and reaching your limit) | On | Any team member with the toggle on |
| Weekly summary | Off | Any team member with the toggle on |
| Invoice ready | On | Organization Admin or Billing Admin |
| Payment failed (a manual payment attempt was declined) | On | Organization Admin or Billing Admin |

New volunteer notifications arrive as a batched digest. The first volunteer matched after a 60-minute cooldown expires sends a digest of everything accumulated since your last email, and further leads within that window keep accumulating for the next digest. This is to prevent spam.

If your organization collaborates on a study, you can receive new-lead and maximum-matched-leads notifications for leads visible to you, such as leads at your own sites or unassigned leads.

### Notices you can't turn off

Certain notifications are always sent with no option to disable them, as they report relevant account or appointment status:

- Notification when a confirmed visit is cancelled due to site changes
- Notification to a collaborator organization when a shared study is paused because its owning organization's account is not in good standing

Billing notifications regarding failed charges, holds, payment status, and hold resolution are sent automatically to your Organization Admin and Billing Admin.
