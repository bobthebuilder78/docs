Every lead in your portal has exactly one status at a time, organized into nine groups shown as tabs on your Volunteers page.

## Updating a lead's status

Click into any lead and use the status dropdown to move them between statuses. This is for your own tracking. Updating a lead's status has no effect on whether or how new leads are sent to you. Use notes on the lead's history to record why a status changed, since there's no separate reason field beyond each group's Miscellaneous catch-all.

## Scheduled visits

If you use Jaspari's scheduling feature, a few statuses in the Scheduled group behave automatically:

- **Confirmation Pending** — a scheduling link has gone out and we're waiting for the volunteer to pick a time.
- **Confirmed by Jaspari** — the volunteer booked through their own link.
- **Confirmed by Staff** — your team confirmed the visit manually, a front-desk confirmation right before the appointment, or a visit your staff booked by hand.

Once your staff sets a status manually on a lead, or books their visit directly, we treat that lead as staff-managed from then on and won't make further automatic status changes on it.

If a change to one of your sites invalidates an appointment that Jaspari's own booking flow confirmed, that appointment is cancelled automatically and the volunteer is notified. If your staff confirmed or booked it instead, this automatic cancellation doesn't apply. You'll need to handle the reschedule directly, since we have no way to know if you've already handled it another way.

## Email notifications

From **Settings → Notifications**, you can control who on your team gets emailed and when.

| Notification | Default | Who can receive it |
|---|---|---|
| New lead matched | On | Any team member with the toggle on |
| Approaching or reached lead limit | On | Any team member with the toggle on |
| Weekly summary | Off | Any team member with the toggle on |
| Invoice ready | On | Organization Admin or Billing Admin |
| Payment failed | On | Organization Admin or Billing Admin |

New lead notifications are batched, not sent one per volunteer. The first lead after your cooldown period expires sends a digest of everything accumulated since your last email, and further leads in that window keep accumulating instead of sending immediately.

A few notifications, account suspension and a collaborator's study being paused because its owner was suspended, always go to every affected team member. They can't be turned off, since they cover account-level status rather than a preference you'd want to silence.
