Every lead in your portal has exactly one status at a time, organized into nine groups shown as tabs on your Volunteers page.

## Updating a lead's status

Click into any lead and use the status dropdown to move them between statuses. This is for your own tracking. Updating a lead's status has no effect on whether or how new leads are sent to you. Use notes on the lead's history to record why a status changed, since there's no separate reason field beyond each group's Miscellaneous catch-all.

## Scheduled visits

When scheduling is enabled, several statuses in the Scheduled group update automatically:

- **Confirmation Pending**: a scheduling link has gone out and we're waiting for the volunteer to pick a time.
- **Confirmed Automatically**: the volunteer completed their booking through the automated system.
- **Confirmed by Staff**: your team confirmed the visit manually, a front-desk confirmation right before the appointment, or a visit your staff booked by hand.

Once your staff sets a status manually on a lead or books a visit directly, that lead is treated as staff-managed. Further status changes are then controlled by your team.

If a site change invalidates an appointment that was automatically booked, that appointment is cancelled and the volunteer is notified. Appointments confirmed or booked by your staff remain under your control for any needed rescheduling.

## Email notifications

From **Settings → Notifications**, you can control who on your team gets emailed and when, for these five types:

| Notification | Default | Who can receive it |
|---|---|---|
| New lead matched | On | Any team member with the toggle on |
| Maximum matched leads (covers both approaching and reaching your limit) | On | Any team member with the toggle on |
| Weekly summary | Off | Any team member with the toggle on |
| Invoice ready | On | Organization Admin or Billing Admin |
| Payment failed (a manual payment attempt was declined) | On | Organization Admin or Billing Admin |

New lead notifications arrive as a batched digest. The first lead after a 60-minute cooldown expires sends a digest of everything accumulated since your last email, and further leads within that window keep accumulating for the next digest.

If your organization collaborates on a study, you can receive new-lead and maximum-matched-leads notifications for leads visible to you, such as leads at your own sites or unassigned leads.

### Notices you can't turn off

Certain notifications are always sent with no option to disable them, as they report critical account or appointment status:

- Notification when a collaborated study is paused by its owner
- Notification when a confirmed visit is cancelled due to site changes

Billing notifications regarding failed charges, holds, payment status, and hold resolution are sent automatically to your Organization Admin and Billing Admin.
