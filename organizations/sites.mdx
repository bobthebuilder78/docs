A site represents one of your physical clinic locations.

## Sites belong to one organization

Each site belongs to exactly one organization. Sites are never shared across organizations, even when a study is shared. If your organization collaborates on someone else's study, you can only ever attach your own sites to it.

## Adding a site to a study

Your organization needs access to a study, as owner or as an invited collaborator at Manager tier, before you can attach one of your own sites to it. Assigning or removing a site requires Organization Admin or Organization Manager access.

A study can have zero, one, or several sites. If the study is shared, those sites can belong to different collaborating organizations at once.

## Turning off Auto-Scheduling at a site

For sites that have Auto-Scheduling on for a particular study you can turn off Auto-Scheduling at any time. If a volunteer is mid-booking there when you turn it off, you'll see a preview of how many people are affected before confirming. See [Auto-Scheduling Visits](/studies/auto-scheduling).

Any open booking flow for all volunteers is released immediately, which makes that volunteer visible to you right away, and they're notified that someone from your team will follow up directly. The platform leaves re-offering affected appointments to your team.

## If a site's setup changes

If a site changes its operating hours outside of currently scheduled appointments' windows, is deactivated, or no longer has enough slots per a period, this may affect existing appointment. A warning indicates that you should review the site's hours and slot configuration.

## Removing a site from an active study

If your recruitment area is defined by radius around your sites, you can't remove the last site from a live study, since it would leave no measurable recruitment area. Add a replacement site first, or switch your recruitment area type before removing the last one.

If removing this site disables scheduling for the study entirely, confirmation is required before proceeding.

## Reassigning a volunteer's site

If a volunteer isn't yet tied to a site (common for hybrid or nationwide studies), you can assign one manually from the volunteer's detail page. This requires at least Team Member access, and the target site has to be one of your own that is already running that study.

Site reassignment cannot be changed while a volunteer has an active or confirmed appointment. The appointment must be cancelled or completed first.
