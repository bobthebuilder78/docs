A site represents one of your physical clinic locations.

## Sites belong to one organization

Each site belongs to exactly one organization. Sites are never shared across organizations, even when a study is shared. If your organization collaborates on someone else's study, you can only ever attach your own sites to it.

## Adding a site to a study

Your organization needs access to a study, as owner or as an invited collaborator at Manager tier, before you can attach one of your own sites to it. Assigning or removing a site requires Organization Admin or Organization Manager access.

A study can have zero, one, or several sites. If the study is shared, those sites can belong to different collaborating organizations at once.

## Turning off scheduling at a location

You can turn off online scheduling at any site at any time, whether or not it's currently working correctly. If a volunteer is mid-booking there when you turn it off, you'll see a preview of who's affected before confirming. Any open appointment hold for that volunteer is released immediately, which makes that lead visible to you right away, and they're notified that someone from your team will follow up directly. The platform leaves re-offering to your team, who follows up and books manually if a different location can still take them.

## If a location's setup changes unexpectedly

If a site loses hours, is deactivated, or has slot configuration issues, the Setup page toggle remains "on" but displays a warning separate from normal "on and working" and "off" states. This warning indicates you should review the site's hours and slot configuration.

## Removing a site from a live study

If your recruitment area is defined by radius around your sites, you can't remove the last site from a live study, since it would leave no measurable recruitment area. Add a replacement site first, or switch your recruitment area type before removing the last one.

If removing this site disables scheduling for the study entirely, confirmation is required before proceeding.

## Reassigning a volunteer's site

If a volunteer isn't yet tied to a site, common for hybrid or nationwide studies, you can assign one manually from the lead's detail page. This requires at least Team Member access, and the target site has to be one of your own, already running that study.

Site re-assignment cannot be changed while a volunteer has an active or confirmed appointment. The appointment must be cancelled or completed first.
