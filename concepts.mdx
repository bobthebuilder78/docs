Jaspari Studies connects clinical research teams with participants through a structured, self-service workflow. Each concept below links to a dedicated guide.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Study" icon="clipboard-list" href="/studies/overview">
    A study is the central unit of recruitment. It defines what you're recruiting for, which participants are eligible, where they must be located, and your pricing and lead limits. Every study belongs to exactly one owning organization.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Eligibility Parameters" icon="filter" href="/studies/eligibility-parameters">
    Eligibility parameters define who qualifies for your study, built from conditions, medications, and structured criteria like age or lab values. Ordinary rules of the same type mean "any one of these"; group rules when you need more than one at once.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Geographic Scope" icon="map-location-dot" href="/studies/geographic-scope">
    Geographic scope controls where volunteers must be located: a radius around a site, a ZIP list, one or more states, or nationwide. Set once per study.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Enrollment Goals" icon="bullseye" href="/studies/enrollment-goals">
    An enrollment goal is an informational target for your own tracking. Max leads and soft limit are the study-level settings that actually cap and wind down lead flow.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Study Formats" icon="layer-group" href="/studies/study-formats">
    Every study is site-based, virtual, or hybrid. What actually determines scheduling behavior is whether you've assigned sites to the study, more than the label itself.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Organization & Roles" icon="users-gear" href="/organizations/roles-permissions">
    Organizations own studies. Five roles control what a team member can do, and access can be further scoped to specific studies and sites.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Billing & Wallet" icon="wallet" href="/billing/how-pricing-works">
    Each study has its own per-lead price, set by formula and locked at launch. You're billed when a volunteer accepts an offer and when a visit is booked. Your wallet is checked first on each invoice, with your card covering any shortfall.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Audit Log" icon="shield-halved" href="/compliance/audit-log">
    Organization Admins can review a log of account activity from organization settings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How the pieces fit together

Your organization signs up and gets approved. A team member creates a study, selects a delivery format, and builds eligibility criteria from conditions, medications, and structured rules. They set a geographic scope and, optionally, sites. Once launched, the platform matches volunteers against those criteria within that geography. You're billed when a volunteer accepts an offer, plus a flat fee for any visit booked through the platform. You can pause, resume, or adjust most settings at any time; a small set of identity fields lock permanently once you launch.
