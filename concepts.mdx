Jaspari Studies connects clinical research teams with pre-qualified participants through a structured, self-service workflow. Understanding these core concepts will help you design studies efficiently, manage teams securely, and stay compliant. Each concept below links to a dedicated guide with step-by-step instructions.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Study" icon="clipboard-list" href="/studies/overview">
    A study is the central unit of recruitment. It defines what you are recruiting for, which participants are eligible, where they must be located, and how many you need. Every study belongs to an organization and supports scoped team access.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Eligibility Parameters" icon="filter" href="/studies/eligibility-parameters">
    Eligibility parameters define who qualifies for your study. Use 4,000+ conditions, medications, and numeric criteria with boolean logic (for example, (A and B) or (A and not Y)) to match your protocol precisely.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Geographic Scope" icon="map-location-dot" href="/studies/geographic-scope">
    Geographic scope controls where volunteers must be located. Set a ZIP radius, expand to statewide coverage, or recruit nationwide. Scope is configured per study, so each trial can target the right population.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Enrollment Goals" icon="bullseye" href="/studies/enrollment-goals">
    Enrollment goals are per-site targets that define how many participants each location must recruit. The platform auto-routes volunteers to under-target sites to keep enrollment balanced across your network.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Study Formats" icon="layer-group" href="/studies/study-formats">
    Jaspari Studies supports standard in-person, hybrid, fully virtual/decentralized, and survey formats. Choose the format that matches your protocol and participant experience requirements.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Organization & Roles" icon="users-gear" href="/organizations/roles-permissions">
    Organizations own studies and user accounts. Roles and permissions are scoped per user, role, study, and site, so team members and external collaborators see only what they are authorized to access.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Wallet & Credits" icon="wallet" href="/billing/wallet-credits">
    The wallet and credits model lets you fund recruitment study by study. Pricing is formula-driven per study based on criteria, geography, and features. There is no minimum spend, no contract length, and no termination fee.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Audit Log" icon="shield-halved" href="/compliance/audit-log">
    The full timestamped audit log captures logins, record views, permission changes, and study sharing. It supports 21 CFR Part 11 and sponsor audits, giving you a complete compliance trail.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How the Pieces Fit Together

A typical lifecycle looks like this. First, your organization signs up and executes the BAA. Next, a team member creates a study, selects a format, and builds eligibility parameters using conditions, medications, and boolean logic. Then they set geographic scope (ZIP radius to nationwide) and assign per-site enrollment goals. Once launched, the platform screens volunteers, matches them against criteria, and auto-routes them to sites that are below target. You monitor progress, adjust scope or goals if needed, and review the audit log for compliance. Credits from your wallet are consumed as recruitment runs, and you can add funds at any time without contracts or termination penalties.
