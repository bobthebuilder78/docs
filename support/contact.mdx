Jaspari Studies provides two support channels for every organization: a dedicated liaison who knows your studies, and a support team available 6 days a week. This page explains how to reach each channel and what to include so your request is resolved quickly.

## Your Dedicated Liaison

Every organization on Jaspari Studies is assigned a dedicated liaison. Your liaison is familiar with your studies, enrollment goals, and team structure. They can help with study setup, eligibility strategy, IRB library questions, billing, and compliance. For ongoing or complex questions, your liaison is often the fastest path to a complete answer.

## Support Team

The support team is available 6 days a week to handle technical issues, platform questions, and account requests. Reach the team by email at any time. Response times vary by issue type, but most inquiries receive a first reply within one business day.

## What to Include

When you contact support, include the following details to speed up resolution:

- **Study name** (or names) affected by the issue
- **Screenshots** showing the error, unexpected state, or relevant UI
- **Timestamps** of when the issue occurred
- **Expected vs. actual behavior** (what you wanted to happen, and what actually happened)
