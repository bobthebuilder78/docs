<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is pricing determined?">
    Pricing is calculated per study rather than a flat rate, based on your eligibility criteria, recruitment geography, and any scheduling features you enable. You'll see a live estimate while setting up a study, before you launch it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the platform support multifactorial eligibility criteria?">
    Yes. You can combine conditions, medications, and structured criteria with inclusion and exclusion logic. Listing several medications or conditions as ordinary rules means a volunteer qualifies by having any one of them. Use the grouping option when you need someone to match more than one at once.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can eligibility criteria be changed mid-study?">
    Yes, most fields stay editable while a study is in draft, active, or paused. A handful of fields (name, study type, delivery model, phase, protocol number) lock the moment a study leaves draft, since changing them after launch would apply retroactively to volunteers already matched under the old definition.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a study be collaborated on by more than one organization?">
    Yes. The owning organization can invite other organizations, a CRO, an individual clinic, a sponsor, to collaborate on a study at Viewer, Recruiter, or Manager tier. Ownership and billing stay with the organization that owns the study throughout. See [Collaboration](/organizations/collaboration) for the full model.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the platform support decentralized or fully virtual studies?">
    Yes. A study's delivery model can be site-based, virtual, or hybrid. A virtual study takes no site assignment. A hybrid study can launch and run with zero sites attached, as long as its recruitment area isn't defined by a radius around a site.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can we run recruitment for studies outside the US?">
    Not currently. Recruitment channels, targeting, and compliance processes are built around US regulatory requirements, so studies and their sites need to be located in the US. Team members themselves can be based anywhere.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if a study needs to change owners?">
    Ownership can transfer from one organization to another, but only to an organization that's already an active collaborator on the study. The new owner needs a payment method on file before accepting. See [Collaboration](/organizations/collaboration) for the full process.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What documentation is available for regulatory or sponsor audits?">
    Every action on the platform is captured in a timestamped audit log: who accessed or viewed a volunteer record, who created or edited data and when, and any change to a role or access level. Organization admins can filter this by team member, action type, and date range.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What support is available if we run into issues?">
    See [Contact](/support/contact) for how to reach support and your dedicated liaison.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does approval take?">
    Organization approval is typically completed within a few business days after you submit your signup request at [studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup). Once approved, you can log in and begin building studies immediately. There is no separate onboarding or implementation cycle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there an onboarding process?">
    No. Jaspari Studies is self-service once your organization is approved. You can create studies, set eligibility parameters, define geographic scope, and launch recruitment without waiting for an implementation team or sales cycle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can sponsors get read-only access?">
    Yes. You can invite sponsors with read-only permissions, including spend monitoring. Sponsors can view recruitment progress and cost data without the ability to modify studies or settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Studies & Eligibility

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What study formats are supported?">
    Jaspari Studies supports every major study format: standard in-person, hybrid, fully virtual and decentralized, and survey-based studies. You select the format during study setup.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many parameters are available?">
    The platform includes more than 4,000 conditions, medications, and numeric criteria. You can combine them with boolean logic (for example, (A and B) or (A and not Y)) to match complex eligibility requirements.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I amend a protocol after launch?">
    Yes. You can update eligibility parameters, geographic scope, and enrollment goals after a study is live. Changes are logged in the audit trail. Depending on the amendment, you may need to notify your IRB or sponsor.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a minimum spend?">
    No. Jaspari Studies has no minimum spend, no contract length, and no termination fee. Pricing is formula-driven per study based on criteria complexity, geography, and features used.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the wallet and credits model work?">
    You add funds to your organization wallet as credits. Costs are drawn down per study based on the formula-driven rate. You can monitor spend in real time and invite sponsors to view read-only spend data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a promotional credit for new organizations?">
    Yes. New organizations launching US studies can receive a \$2,500 credit through the Q4 2026 promotion when they reach enrollment milestones. Contact your liaison for eligibility details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Compliance

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is my data HIPAA compliant?">
    Yes. Jaspari Studies is HIPAA compliant and executes a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) at the organization level. One BAA covers all studies and users under your organization. See [HIPAA & BAA](/compliance/hipaa-baa) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who owns the participant data?">
    Your organization retains ownership of participant data collected for your studies. Jaspari acts as a business associate and processes data on your behalf under the terms of the BAA.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the platform support 21 CFR Part 11?">
    Yes. Jaspari Studies maintains a full timestamped audit log of logins, record views, permission changes, study sharing, and other platform actions. This supports 21 CFR Part 11 and sponsor audit requirements. See [Audit Log](/compliance/audit-log) for more.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Support

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I reach support?">
    Contact the support team at [support@jaspari.com](mailto:support@jaspari.com). Support is available 6 days a week. Every organization also receives a dedicated liaison for study-specific questions. See [Contact Support](/support/contact) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I include in a support request?">
    Include the study name, screenshots, timestamps, and a clear description of expected versus actual behavior. This helps the team diagnose and resolve your issue faster.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
