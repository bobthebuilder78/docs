## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there an onboarding process?">
    No. Jaspari Studies is self-service once your organization is approved. You can create studies, set eligibility criteria, define geographic scope, and launch without an implementation team or sales cycle.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Studies & eligibility

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the platform support multiple eligibility criteria at once?">
    Yes. You can combine conditions, medications, and structured criteria with inclusion and exclusion rules. Listing several medications or conditions as ordinary rules means a volunteer qualifies by having any one of them. Use the grouping option when you need someone to match more than one at once. See [Eligibility Parameters](/studies/eligibility-parameters).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can eligibility criteria be changed mid-study?">
    Yes. Most fields stay editable while a study is in draft, active, or paused. A handful of fields (name, study type, delivery model, phase, protocol number) lock the moment a study leaves draft, permanently.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What study formats are supported?">
    Site-based, virtual, and hybrid. You select the format during study setup, and it locks permanently once you launch. See [Study Formats](/studies/study-formats).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Collaboration

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a study be worked on by more than one organization?">
    Yes. The owning organization can invite other organizations, such as a CRO, an individual clinic, or a sponsor, to collaborate on a study at Viewer, Recruiter, or Manager tier. Ownership and billing stay with the owning organization throughout. See [Collaboration](/organizations/collaboration).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if a study needs to change owners?">
    Ownership can transfer to an organization that's already an active collaborator on the study. The new owner needs a saved payment method, and needs to clear their organization's wallet deposit minimum, before accepting. See [Collaboration](/organizations/collaboration).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is pricing determined?">
    Each study gets its own per-lead price: a base rate plus a surcharge based on how rare your eligibility criteria are and how small your recruitment area's population is. That price locks in when you launch. See [Pricing](/billing/pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What am I actually billed for?">
    Two things: the per-lead price, charged when a volunteer accepts your study, and a flat scheduling fee, charged when a visit is booked through the platform.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the wallet work?">
    You can deposit funds to your organization's wallet at any time. When an invoice is due, your wallet is checked first. Full coverage means no card charge; partial coverage means your card covers the rest. See [Billing Cycles](/billing/billing-cycles) and [Credits](/billing/credits).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Compliance

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is a Business Associate Agreement available?">
    Yes. Organization Admins can review and accept a BAA from organization settings, on their own timeline separate from launching a study. See [HIPAA & BAA](/compliance/hipaa-baa).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there an audit log?">
    Yes, available to Organization Admins from organization settings. See [Audit Log](/compliance/audit-log).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
