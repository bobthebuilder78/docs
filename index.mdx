description: "Clinical research recruitment platform for identifying, screening, and enrolling volunteers."
---

The Studies Platform automates recruitment workflows for clinical trials. Organizations configure eligibility criteria and geographic scope, then the system matches and manages incoming volunteers through enrollment.
