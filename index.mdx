## Your high-level journey

## Getting a study live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create your organization at [https://studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup). Approval is required before login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your geographic scope">
    Scope recruitment from a ZIP radius up to statewide or nationwide coverage, per study.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Activate recruitment. The platform auto-routes volunteers to under-target sites to keep enrollment balanced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor">
    Track progress, review the audit log, and adjust scope or goals as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set recruitment goals and launch">
    Set your enrollment goal and target dates, complete any required compliance steps, and launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>
