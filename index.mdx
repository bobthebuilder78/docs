## Getting a study live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create your organization at [studies.jaspari.com/signup](https://studies.jaspari.com/signup). Approval is required before login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your study">
    Add eligibility criteria and a geographic scope, from a ZIP radius up to statewide or nationwide coverage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your enrollment goal and limits">
    Add an enrollment goal for your own tracking, and set max leads / soft limit if you want to cap or wind down lead flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Activate recruitment. Matching against your criteria begins immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor and adjust">
    Track progress and adjust criteria, geography, or limits as needed. Most settings stay editable after launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>
