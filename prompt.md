Tone & Style Guidelines:Zero Fluff: Eliminate introductory filler, conversational transitions, and concluding summaries. Do not use phrases like "Let's look at," "Next up is," "It's important to note," or "In this section."Interface-First Language: Always ground instructions in the actual UI. Use precise technical verbs (Navigate to, Toggle on, Select, Field, Screen, Status).Parallel Structure: In any list, every single item must start the exact same way (e.g., all starting with a bolded noun or all starting with an active imperative verb).Short, Direct Sentences: Keep sentences brief. Avoid complex, multi-clause sentences. Use clear conditional structures ("If X is true, then Y happens.").Formatting Rules:Scannable Headers: Organize the page using clear H2 (##) and H3 (###) markdown headers.Punchy Lists: Bulleted or numbered lists should be highly condensed fragments, not long paragraphs.Banned Words: Seamlessly, effortlessly, easily, quickly, delve, simplify, powerhouse, dive in, look no further, robust.Structural Blueprint to Emulate:Feature Overview: A 1-2 sentence high-level summary of what the feature/page does.Numbered Steps/Components: A strictly formatted list detailing UI elements or chronological steps.Rules/Constraints: Explicitly state what locks, what errors occur, and what the strict requirements are using clear bullet points. NO EM DASHES AT ALL, NO "-", and do not say "it is Y, not Z".
